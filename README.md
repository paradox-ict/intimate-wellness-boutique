# Intimate Wellness Boutique

Create a full-stack, production-ready single-product e-commerce website for Vaginal Tightening Gel (intimate care herbal gel) targeted at the Pakistani market. Leave the brand name, brand colors, and visual identity open for creative exploration without strict branding limits, while keeping the tone tasteful, trustworthy, and discreet.

Design & User Experience

• Build a unique, modern, sleek, and visually appealing layout with high-end UI/UX suited for an intimate wellness product.

• Include rich, smooth animations and micro-interactions throughout the shopping journey.

• Design with a mobile-first approach, ensuring the site is fully responsive and performs seamlessly across all devices (mobile, tablet, and desktop).

Core Features & Functionality

• Storefront & Product Showcase: High-converting landing page highlighting the Vaginal Tightening Gel, featuring its key benefits (antibacterial, cleansing, moisturizing, tightening, anti-itching, odor removal, lubricating), natural/herbal formulation, CE & ISO 9001 certifications, step-by-step usage instructions with applicator details, bundle options (single piece vs. multi-pack), FAQs, and customer reviews.

• Privacy & Trust Elements: Highlight discreet packaging and confidential delivery to build buyer confidence.

• Cart & Checkout Flow: Streamlined, single-page or minimal-step checkout optimized for mobile users.

• Payment Methods (Pakistan-Focused):

    • Cash on Delivery (COD).

    • Easypaisa manual transfer integration, including an option for customers to upload/submit payment proof (receipt screenshot/transaction ID) during checkout.

• Backend & Admin Panel:

    • Full-stack architecture to manage orders, inventory, bundle pricing, and customer details.

    • Payment verification workflow in the admin dashboard to review submitted Easypaisa payment proofs and approve or reject orders.

    • Order status tracking for both admins and customers.
